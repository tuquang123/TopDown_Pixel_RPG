Boss audio sources added for the default boss sound profile:

- 80 CC0 RPG SFX by rubberduck, OpenGameArt, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
- CC0 Deep Monster Roar by trazzz123, OpenGameArt, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-deep-monster-roar

Runtime data lives in Resources/Audio/Boss/BossSoundData.json.
Audio clips are loaded by Resources path, so future boss profiles can be added by creating a new bossId entry and pointing each event to more clips under Resources.
